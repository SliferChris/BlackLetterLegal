ABOUT BLACK LETTER LEGAL

Clearer Law. Stronger Writing. More Confidence.

Black Letter Legal was created to give law students the kind of academic support that goes beyond simply explaining the law.

Meet Aysha

I am an LLB (Hons) law graduate with postgraduate study at LLM level and practical experience within the Scottish legal system.

My tutoring focuses on helping students understand not only what the law is, but how to apply it, structure an argument and communicate legal analysis clearly.

Black Letter Legal developed from a genuine interest in making difficult legal concepts easier to understand. What began as helping other students work through challenging topics has developed into tailored academic support for students at different stages of their legal education.

Sessions are structured, practical and adapted to the individual student. Whether the focus is an essay, problem question, exam preparation or simply understanding a difficult area of law, the aim is always the same: to build stronger legal reasoning and greater academic confidence.

Clarity

Complex legal principles broken down into clear, manageable explanations.

Structure

Support with organising legal arguments, essays and problem questions effectively.

Confidence

Helping students develop their own legal reasoning rather than relying on model answers.
